4 Target Tumor Categories

Class 01

Glioma

Intra-axial glial cell neoplasms. Characterized by infiltration into surrounding brain parenchyma and varied vascularity.

Class 02

Meningioma

Extra-axial tumors arising from the arachnoid layer of the meninges covering the brain and spinal cord.

Class 03

Pituitary

Sellar/parasellar region tumors that may alter hormone regulation or cause optical chiasm compression.

Class 04

No Tumor

Absence of hallmark morphological tumor patterns on the provided single-slice 2D MRI study.
